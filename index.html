Why We Say It Uploader

The tool that publishes videos to the Why We Say It YouTube channel, which tells the stories behind everyday sayings.

What it does

Why We Say It Uploader is a small program that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It does two things with the channel's Google account:

  • Uploads videos. It sends each finished video to the Why We Say It channel with its title, description and tags.
  • Reads the channel's analytics. It reads how the channel's own videos performed (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares and audience retention) so the owner can see which videos work best.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Why We Say It channel. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not collect information from viewers or anyone else.

Why it asks for Google permissions

  • Manage your YouTube videos (upload): needed to publish videos to the channel.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports: needed to read the performance figures for the channel's own videos.

It asks for nothing else, and uses these permissions only for the two purposes above. Details are in the privacy policy.
