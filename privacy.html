Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Why We Say It Uploader ("the app") is a program the owner of the Why We Say It YouTube channel runs on their own computer to publish videos to that channel and to read that channel's analytics. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

Who the app is for

The app is used only by the channel owner, with the channel's own Google account. It is not offered to the public. It does not collect any information from viewers of the channel, visitors to this website, or any other person.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives:

  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google, which let the app act for the channel without knowing the account's password.
  • Upload results from YouTube: the ID, link and privacy status of each video the app uploads.
  • Analytics for the channel's own videos from the YouTube Analytics API: views, average view duration and percentage, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained and audience retention. These are totals; they do not identify individual viewers.

The app does not access email, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

How the information is used

  • Sign-in tokens are used only to upload videos to the channel and to request the channel's analytics.
  • Upload results are kept as a record of what has been posted.
  • Analytics are used only by the channel owner to understand how videos perform and to decide what to make next.

Where it is stored

All of this is stored in files in the channel owner's own private storage: their computer and their personal cloud backup of it. The app has no server and no database of its own.

Sharing

Information obtained from Google is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone else. The only parties that receive data from the app are Google and YouTube themselves, when the app uploads a video or requests analytics.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting information

Stored information is kept for as long as the channel owner uses the app, and is removed by deleting the app's files. Analytics held by the app are refreshed from YouTube each time they are requested rather than kept as a long-term archive.

The app's access can be withdrawn at any time on Google's security settings page, after which its sign-in tokens stop working.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. The app uses none either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel's About page.
